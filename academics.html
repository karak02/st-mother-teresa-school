OUR EDUCATORS

Teachers – Mentors & Facilitators

Teachers are the guiding force behind every learner's educational journey. At St. Mother Teresa International School, educators strive to create classrooms that are intellectually stimulating, emotionally secure and conducive to exploration.

Our teachers endeavour to move beyond the conventional role of instructors and become mentors, facilitators, motivators and role models. Through continuous professional development and reflective teaching practices, educators remain responsive to the evolving needs of learners and the changing landscape of education.
