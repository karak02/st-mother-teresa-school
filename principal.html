Principal's Message

Dear Students, Parents and Well-wishers,

At St. Mother Teresa International School, we believe that education is not merely about academic excellence, but about shaping compassionate, confident and responsible individuals who can make a positive difference in the world.

Our vision is to nurture every child’s unique potential and to provide a safe, supportive and inspiring environment where young minds can learn, grow and flourish. With the values of discipline, dedication and service, we strive to build a future-ready generation, rooted in strong moral values and a spirit of kindness.

“Together, let us walk hand in hand in this journey of learning, growth and success. Because every child matters, and every dream deserves a chance to bloom.”

With warm regards,
DR. MOWPIA G. WILLIAM
Principal, St. Mother Teresa International School
